# 012: Explicit uncertainty budgets, local trial

Use conservative first-order bounds, not statistical confidence intervals. Student
inputs are estimates in original-media units; display zoom cannot alter them.
Calibration is a common multiplicative term and must not be independently counted
for every point in a derivative. A selected origin and orientation are fixed
coordinate conventions in this model. Unassessed effects are stated, not invented.
Blank optional input is unknown and differs from zero. Preserve raw worksheet rows
and transformation/repeat provenance in additive graph-document fields. Excluded
rows remain editable and saved. Do not round source data for presentation.
