## IB mathematics/uncertainty resource audit

Read owner's Physics_IB_first-assessmente-2028.pdf (26-page print of IB Mathematics
resource, not the complete programme guide). Extracted complete text; inspected
rendered pp.4 and14 for repeated-measurement and max/min-fit guidance. Key locations:
pp.3–7 propagation, precision and tables; pp.8–9 automatic gradients and R²;
pp.10–14 error bars, outliers, physics-led fits, optional linearization and all-point
maximum/minimum gradients; pp.14–19 intercept/interpretation/literature examples;
pp.20–26 video methodology and evaluation.

Code comparison: independent worksheet supports per-row symmetric X/Y errors,
linear/quadratic/sinusoidal fits and all-point worst-fit bounds; worstFit computes
slope uncertainty but no reported intercept uncertainty. Restored media AnalysisPanel
has no worst-fit controls. App velocity rows set error:null; position error uses a
uniform pixel-to-length conversion. No general transform/formula/propagation engine,
repeat-summary workflow, uncertainty-aware rounding, or reversible exclude-with-reason
field. Raw numeric precision is retained in calculations, but result displays use fixed
4 decimals/5 significant digits independently of uncertainty. Graph documents preserve
raw strings but not repeated measurement sets/provenance. Excluding via deleting or
cropping is not an outlier audit trail. Video multi-section CSV is not directly compatible
with the independent 2/4-column parser. Video CSV labels length as m even without scale;
axis labels themselves support pixel units. These are audit findings, not fixes here.

Important distinctions: the source accepts symmetric errors, does not require R² on
figures or advanced statistical analysis, and discusses linearization as useful but not
mandatory in every investigation. Scientific reasoning and methodological evaluation
remain student responsibilities. Recommendation: first share worst-fit UI/logic with
familiar media view, add intercept uncertainty and defensible video uncertainty workflow;
then precision-aware reporting, transforms with propagation, repeat summaries and
reversible justified exclusions. No blanket IB-completeness claim is justified.
